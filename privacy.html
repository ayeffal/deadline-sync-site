Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

deadline-sync is a personal tool used only by its author. It runs on the author's own computer.

What it accesses

  • The list of the author's Google calendars, to find the ones it writes to.
  • Events in those calendars: it creates, changes and deletes only the events it created itself.

What it does with that data

  • Nothing leaves the author's computer except the calls to Google Calendar that make those changes.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or for training any model.
  • The Google sign-in is stored only on the author's computer and can be revoked at any time in the Google account settings, under "Third-party apps & services".

deadline-sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
